🌷 Who We Are

Handmade Blooms is a flower and handmade gifting store based in Pali, Rajasthan. We bring beautiful handmade flowers and gifting options together in one place, making it easier to find something meaningful for your loved ones.

💐 What We Offer

From individual handmade flowers to custom bouquets, Handmade Blooms gives you the freedom to choose something special. You can explore our flowers, select available colours, and create a gift that matches your occasion and feelings.

❤️ Our Goal

Our goal is to make gifting simple, beautiful and memorable while providing every customer with a pleasant shopping experience. We want every order from Handmade Blooms to feel thoughtful and special.
